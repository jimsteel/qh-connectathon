How the numbers are derived from FHIR 

    
    
      
  • Referral: ServiceRequest with code SNOMED 3457005 (Patient referral), status not
        entered-in-error/revoked/draft; counted in the quarter of authoredOn.
    • 
      
  • Waitlisted case: ServiceRequest with a category from the elective-category code system
        (the surgical waitlist entry), status not entered-in-error; counted in the quarter of authoredOn (date placed on the list).
    • 
      
  • Category of a referral: the category of the waitlist entry the referral points to with
        supportingInfo; failing that, the patient's most recent waitlist entry authored on or before the referral.
    • 
      
  • Diagnosis of a referral: the patient's Condition (SNOMED CT) with the earliest recordedDate on or after
        the referral date (else the latest one before it). Conditions without a code show as "Uncoded"; no Condition at all as
        "No anaemia diagnosis" (screened and cleared).
    • 
      
  • Quarters: Australian financial year (1 July – 30 June); "2025–26 Q1" is Jul–Sep 2025. "All" spans the first to the
        last quarter with a referral; waitlist entries authored outside that span are not shown.
