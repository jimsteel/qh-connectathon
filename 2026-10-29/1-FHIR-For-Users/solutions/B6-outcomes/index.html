Per-patient values
How the numbers are derived from FHIR

Three searches (all pages are followed):


    
    
      
  • Results: Observation with LOINC 718-7 (haemoglobin, g/L) or 2276-4 (ferritin, µg/L), status not
        entered-in-error/cancelled, value from valueQuantity (g/dL is converted to g/L),
        date from effectiveDateTime (or effectivePeriod.start, else issued). Sex from the included
        Patient's gender.
    • 
      
  • PAM cohort: patients with an IV iron MedicationRequest or a PAM EpisodeOfCare (type episode-type|pam).
        Patients with results but neither are left out (counted in the status line).
    • 
      
  • IV iron: MedicationRequest whose dosageInstruction.route is SNOMED 47625008 (intravenous) or whose
        medication is one of the AMT IV iron products, status not cancelled / entered-in-error / draft.
        The treatment date is the earliest such authoredOn.
    • 
      
  • Initial = the patient's latest result on or before the treatment date. Post-treatment = the latest result
        after it. Patients not given IV iron are anchored on their PAM referral (EpisodeOfCare.period.start).
        The alternative method ("earliest vs latest") is what the starter dashboard does.
    • 
      
  • Thresholds: anaemia Hb <120 g/L (female) / <130 g/L (male); ferritin <100 µg/L (the track's draft decision rules).
    • 
      
  • Timeframe applies to the treatment date (or referral date) — Australian financial years.
